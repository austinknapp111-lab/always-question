04

Who it's for — and what we're not

The 8-year / 100,000-mile line

Reviews are for cars 8 years old or newer and under 100,000 miles. That's not arbitrary — it's where the coverage lives: emissions warranties, customer programs, recent parts warranties, and documented-defect arguments all sit inside that window.

Over the line? You're accepted only if you confirm in writing at intake that you know you'll be paying and no warranty applies. Then the job changes honestly: no coverage hunt — just the savings hunt and the Best Path Memo. No false hope sold.

What we are not

  • Not lawyers, and this isn't legal advice.
  • Not mechanics selling you repairs — we sell the read, nothing else.
  • We don't decide warranty. The manufacturer decides. We read, we classify, we show our work.
  • No outcome is guaranteed. The documents are the product.
  • The review is documents-only. We tell you exactly what to say to the dealer, and show you the page it's on.
